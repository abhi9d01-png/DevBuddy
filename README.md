
# 🤖 DevBuddy

### Your Personal AI-Powered Coding Companion

DevBuddy is a learning companion designed to help beginners learn programming, practise coding concepts, track their progress, and revise topics through interactive learning.

## ✨ Features

- 📚 **Learning Roadmap** — Follow a structured path from programming basics to real-world projects.
- 🧠 **Practice & Quiz Mode** — Test your knowledge with programming questions and learn from your mistakes.
- 📈 **Progress Tracker** — Track completed topics, confidence levels, and learning progress.
- 🔄 **Smart Revision** — Revisit previous mistakes and strengthen your understanding.
- 💻 **Project-Based Learning** — Apply your knowledge by building practical projects.
- 🤖 **AI-Assisted Learning** — Get explanations and learning support through integrated AI features.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Choose a programming topic.
2. Learn the fundamentals step by step.
3. Practise with questions and quizzes.
4. Review feedback and understand your mistakes.
5. Track your progress and identify weak areas.
6. Revise previous topics to improve retention.
7. Apply your knowledge to real-world projects.

## 🎯 Who Is It For?

- Beginners learning to code.
- Students practising programming.
- Self-taught developers.
- Anyone who wants a structured coding learning experience.
